# Industry Workflows

## Shared foundation

All profiles use the same customers/suppliers, items/services, sales, purchases, banking, accounting, VAT, inventory, projects/jobs, files, documents, email, reporting, users, and permissions.

## Technical Services

Request → site/equipment inspection → quotation → acceptance → work order → visits and technicians → labour/material/expense/subcontract capture → variation → completion evidence and signature → service report → invoice → receipt → warranty/service history.

## Automotive Workshop

Customer/vehicle → intake and condition evidence → diagnosis → estimate and selective approval → job card → technician labour and parts → outside work → additional-work approval → quality check → invoice/payment → vehicle release → warranty/comeback and service history.

## Civil and Architectural Services

Enquiry/site → proposal and revisions → project/contract → phases, milestones, tasks, and deliverables → time, purchases, materials, expenses, and subcontract costs → variations → progress/milestone billing → receipts → snag/closeout → final profitability.

## Profile rule

Profiles may supply terminology, default roles, fields, templates, checklists, chart additions, dashboards, and reports. They may not fork accounting, duplicate master data, bypass access control, or hardcode commercial plans.
